Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy covers Forged Daily Uploader (the "App"), a private tool operated by Forged Daily. The App is used only by its owner to publish Forged Daily's own videos to its own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not available to the public.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the App accesses

  • YouTube: with the account owner's permission, the App uploads videos to the owner's channel, updates the titles and descriptions of those videos, and reads basic channel information (name, video count) to confirm the right channel is connected.
  • Instagram: with the account owner's permission, the App publishes Reels to the owner's account and reads basic account information (username, post count).

The App does not access, collect or store data about any other users, viewers or commenters.

How data is used and stored

The only data stored is the authorization tokens needed to publish on the owner's behalf. They are kept privately and are never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google security settings or from Instagram's Settings → Apps and websites. When access is revoked or upon request, the stored tokens are deleted within 7 days.
